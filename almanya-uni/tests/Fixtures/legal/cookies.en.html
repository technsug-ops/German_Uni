What is a cookie?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser, used to keep services working and gather statistics.

Which cookies do we use?

  • Essential cookies: Session, CSRF protection, dil tercihi. Onay gerektirmez (GDPR 6(1)(f)).
  • Analytics cookies: Anonim ziyaretçi sayımı. Sadece onayınızla etkinleştirilir.
  • Marketing cookies: Yok. Üçüncü taraf takip kullanmıyoruz.

Your control

On first visit you see a cookie banner. Use Accept or Reject. You can change your preference anytime by clearing cookies.
