**Short answer:** For a university application, what matters is not the exam brand but the type and level of the certificate. The joint framework regulation of the HRK and the KMK lists **telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule** and **Goethe-Zertifikat C2: GDS** as exempting certificates. **Goethe-Zertifikat C1** and the general **telc Deutsch C1** are not on that list; whether they are accepted depends on the university and the programme.

## How Goethe and telc differ for your application

| Certificate | In the framework regulation | What to check for your programme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule | Exempting certificate | Whether it is on the programme's list of accepted certificates |
| telc Deutsch C1 (general) | Not listed | Whether the university accepts it separately |
| Goethe-Zertifikat C2: GDS | Exempting certificate | The programme's rule if you hold an older Goethe diploma |
| Goethe-Zertifikat C1 | Not listed | Up to the university; RWTH Aachen accepts it, for example, while LMU München lists only Goethe C2 |

telc offers two C1 exams: **telc Deutsch C1** is for general purposes, **telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule** is designed for university entrance. Check on the programme page which one it accepts for applications.

## At application or at enrolment?

Whether the certificate is due at application or only by enrolment depends on the programme. Meeting the language requirement does not mean you meet the programme's other admission requirements.

Before you book an exam, check the list of certificates your target university accepts. Details and official sources: [German level and language certificates for university](/en/blog/goethe-telc-testdaf-dsh-difference-german-language-exam-comparison-for-turkish-en)
