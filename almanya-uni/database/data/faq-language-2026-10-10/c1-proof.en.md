**Short answer:** You prove C1 German for university with an exam certificate. Under the joint framework regulation of the HRK and the KMK, **TestDaF** with at least **TDN 4** in all four parts and **DSH-2** count as proof for all programmes, and **telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule** is listed as an exempting certificate. Which certificate and which result are accepted is decided by the university and the programme.

## Certificates used for C1

| Certificate | Place in the framework regulation | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TestDaF (TDN 4 in every part) | Proof for all programmes | A university can set a different level for a specific programme |
| DSH-2 | Proof for all programmes | Offered by universities and recognised Studienkollegs in Germany; a DSH registered with the HRK is recognised by German universities |
| telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule | Exempting certificate | Individual universities can have different rules |
| Goethe-Zertifikat C1 | Not listed | Up to the university; RWTH Aachen accepts it, for example, while LMU München lists only Goethe C2 |
| ÖSD Zertifikat C1 | Not listed | Up to the university |

At C2 level, **Goethe-Zertifikat C2: GDS** and **ÖSD C2** are also listed as exempting certificates in the framework regulation.

## When do you hand it in?

Whether the certificate is due at application or only by enrolment depends on the programme. A course attendance certificate does not replace an exam result. Meeting the language requirement does not mean you meet the programme's other admission requirements.
